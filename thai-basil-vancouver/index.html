Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Vancouver

Located right on Cornwall Avenue, Thai Basil brings together time-honored Thai culinary traditions and the fresh ingredients of the West Coast.

Every dish is balanced with care—blending aromatic lemongrass, fiery bird's eye chilies, sweet Thai basil, and rich coconut milk to create memorable flavors for neighborhood diners and visitors alike.
