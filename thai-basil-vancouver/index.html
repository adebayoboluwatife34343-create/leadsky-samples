Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Vancouver

Located right on Cornwall Avenue, Thai Basil brings together time-honored Thai culinary traditions and the fresh ingredients of the West Coast.

Every dish is balanced with care—blending aromatic lemongrass, fiery bird's eye chilies, sweet Thai basil, and rich coconut milk to create memorable flavors for neighborhood diners and visitors alike.

Artisanal Heritage

The Art of Thai Spice & Herb Harmony

At Thai Basil on Cornwall Ave, every dish tells a story of heritage and precision. We combine time-honoured culinary traditions from Thailand with Vancouver's vibrant West Coast energy.

From pounding fresh lemongrass and galangal in traditional brass mortars to balancing the five core tastes of sweet, spicy, sour, salty, and bitter, our kitchen honours authentic craftsmanship.
