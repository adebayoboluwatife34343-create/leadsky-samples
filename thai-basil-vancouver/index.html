Cornwall Ave Heritage

Rooted in Authentic Street-Kitchen Traditions

Located at 2184 Cornwall Avenue in Vancouver, Thai Basil brings together generations of culinary expertise. Every dish is a tribute to the bustling street food markets of Thailand, crafted with uncompromising dedication to authentic flavor profiles.

We believe great Thai cuisine begins with uncompromising freshness—from hand-pounded curry pastes in our traditional mortar and pestle to premium herbs and spices delivered daily.
