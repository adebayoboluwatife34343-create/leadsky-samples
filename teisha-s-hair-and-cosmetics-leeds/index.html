04 Before you get in touch

A good chat
starts here.

Make the conversation about you. These simple details can help explain what you are looking for.

  1. Tell them your goal.

    A different look, help choosing products, or simply a conversation about your options.

  2. Describe your starting point.

    Mention your current style and routine, and anything you would like the salon to consider.

  3. Ask for the practical details.

    Confirm suitable services, prices, appointment availability and opening hours directly.

Does this quiz book an appointment?

No. This is an independent website concept. The quiz creates a conversation starter only. Call Teisha’s on 0113 307 0077 to discuss appointments. Nothing is sent through this page.

Can I see prices or opening hours here?

Current prices and opening hours have not been provided for this concept. Please ask the salon directly before making plans or travelling.

Are these photographs the salon’s work?

No. All photographs shown here are licensed stock references. They do not show Teisha’s work, staff, customers or premises.
