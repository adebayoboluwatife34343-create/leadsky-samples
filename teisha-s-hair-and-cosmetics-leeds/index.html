Before you visit

A few useful details.

Can I book through this website?

This is an independent website concept. You can try the enquiry drawer, but it does not send messages or reserve appointments. To arrange an appointment, call Teisha’s on 0113 307 0077.

Where can I find treatment prices?

A current, verified price list has not been supplied for this concept. Please contact the salon to confirm the treatment you want, its price and any preparation needed.

Are these photographs of Teisha’s clients?

No. The photographs are licensed stock images used to show the design direction. They do not show Teisha’s staff, premises, products or completed work.

How do I find the salon?

The supplied business address is Unit 82, Merrion Centre, Leeds LS2 8NG. Use the directions link above to open a map. Please call the salon to confirm opening hours before travelling.
