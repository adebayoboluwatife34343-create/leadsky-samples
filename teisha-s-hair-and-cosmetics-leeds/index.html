Before you visit
How do I book a real appointment?

Call Teisha’s Hair and Cosmetics on 0113 307 0077. The salon can confirm the service, price and availability. The booking form on this independent concept does not send an enquiry or reserve an appointment.

Where is the salon?

Teisha’s Hair and Cosmetics is at Unit 82, Merrion Centre, Leeds LS2 8NG. Use the directions link to open the address in Google Maps. Please confirm opening hours by phone before travelling.

Can I buy products on this page?

No purchases or payments are taken here. You can try the enquiry list to explore the concept. Contact the salon directly for its current products, prices and stock.

Are these photos of the salon’s work?

No. All photographs are licensed stock images used for visual inspiration. They do not show Teisha’s staff, premises, products or completed hairstyles. Original illustrations are also used.

What are the opening hours?

Opening hours have not been verified for this concept. Please call 0113 307 0077 to check when the salon is open and arrange your visit.
