A little bakery in Cork

Cameron Bakery is a neighbourhood stop in Blackrock Village. We bake bread and pastries each morning for people who like a proper crust, a flaky layer, or something sweet to go with coffee.

Call ahead to ask what is available today, or drop by during opening hours. Every photograph on this page is illustrative stock photography used to show a possible design direction.
