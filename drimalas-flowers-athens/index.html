04 / Good to know

Before

Where is Drimalas Flowers?

The supplied business address is Ηρώων Πολυτεχνείου 86, Πειραιάς 18536, Greece. Open the address in Google Maps to check your route. Confirm the location with the shop before travelling.

What time is the shop open?

Opening hours have not been supplied for this independent concept. Call +30 698 907 8351 to confirm opening times for your chosen day. Holiday and exceptional opening times also need direct confirmation.

Can I check flowers before visiting?

Yes, you can contact the shop to ask what is available. Call +30 698 907 8351 or email flowersdrimalas@yahoo.gr. The photographs here are stock floral references and do not represent the shop’s current selection.

Does the planner book an appointment?

No. Selecting a date only changes your personal visit plan on this page. Nothing is sent, and no appointment, order or reservation is made. Contact the shop directly to make any arrangements.
