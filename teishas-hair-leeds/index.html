The salon

A flagship salon in the heart of Leeds.

Teisha's was founded by professional stylist Nateisha Dewdney and opened its flagship salon at the Merrion Centre in 2020, in Unit 82 facing the Arena Way mall.

It's a place for Afro-Caribbean hair: styled in the chair, with the products to look after it at home.
