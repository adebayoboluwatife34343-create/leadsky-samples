Tradition Fused With Precision

Located on West 73rd Avenue in Vancouver, Café de l'Orangerie offers a tranquil sanctuary where classic French patisserie meets the subtle artistry of Japanese culinary standards.

Every creation is carefully prepared using choice ingredients, bringing together the delicate textures of traditional Parisian baking with Japanese dedication to balance, beauty, and refined sweetness.
