01 / From inspiration to enquiry

Your next look,
step by step.

A little preparation makes a useful conversation. Bring your inspiration, explain what matters to you and ask the studio to confirm the details.

01

Start with your idea.

Describe the look you have in mind. Include the occasion and any date you are working towards.

02

Talk through the details.

Share your starting point and reference photos. Ask which options the studio offers for your request.

03

Agree before you book.

Confirm the service, total price, what is included and how much appointment time to allow.

04

Confirm your visit.

Ask the studio to confirm availability, location and any preparation needed before you arrive.
