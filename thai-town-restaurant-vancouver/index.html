Our Heritage & Craft

A Botanical Sanctuary for Thai Gastronomy

Situated on bustling Granville Street, Thai Town Restaurant brings the vibrant street markets and royal kitchens of Thailand to Vancouver. Our culinary philosophy centers on balance: the perfect harmony of sweet, spicy, sour, and salty notes in every dish.

We source fresh aromatics including lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and Thai basil, crafting curry pastes and sauces from scratch daily. Step into our warm teakwood dining room and experience genuine hospitality.
