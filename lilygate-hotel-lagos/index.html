THE PRODUCT, IN THREE STEPS

Fewer hand-offs.
More time for the guest.

A proposed single channel for choosing a room, preparing an arrival and ordering dining. Try the journey, then review everything together.

01 — Choose your stay

Pick dates, compare the real room-category descriptions and select from a clearly labelled sample inventory. Review the rate calculation before creating a local sample reservation.

02 — Arrange the details

Add your arrival preferences and explore a demonstration dining menu. The same sample stay stays with you, so you do not need to select your room again.

03 — Keep one shared record

Review, edit or cancel sample records in My stay. An illustrative loyalty ledger demonstrates how connected guest activity could be presented. No real bookings, orders or points are created.

What would need connecting?

Live availability and room allocation require a PMS integration. Kitchen routing requires POS integration. Payments, loyalty rules and push notifications need approved providers and hotel validation. None are connected here. Current hotel capabilities and any existing loyalty programme remain unverified.
