A few useful details

Before your
appointment.

How do I arrange an appointment?

The supplied scheduling instruction is “DM to Schedule”. Open @hairbyberry26 on TikTok and use the contact options available there. Share your preferred hairstyle and exact date, then ask HAIRBYBERRY to confirm the appointment details.

Where is HAIRBYBERRY based?

A business address and service area have not been verified for this concept. Ask HAIRBYBERRY for the full appointment location and any arrival instructions before making travel plans.

Which styles can I book, and what do they cost?

A service menu and prices have not been verified. Send a reference for your preferred style and ask about availability, length, hair requirements, total price and any payment arrangements. The video previews do not confirm that a particular style is currently offered.

What should I check before the day?

Confirm the exact date and time, address, expected appointment length, hair preparation requirements and whether hair is provided. Ask about any deposit, changes or cancellation terms directly; this concept does not set business policies.

Does the planner make a booking?

No. It creates a message you can copy and send yourself. Selecting a day does not check opening hours, hold a slot, collect payment or confirm an appointment. Your appointment must be agreed directly with HAIRBYBERRY.
