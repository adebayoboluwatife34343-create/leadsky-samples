A few useful details.

When can I visit?

The supplied business details list Monday–Thursday, 17:00–22:00, and Friday–Sunday, 10:00–22:00. These hours have not been independently verified for this concept. Please confirm directly before travelling.

Does my selection book a table or order food?

No. This is an independent demonstration. The selection tool only helps you prepare an enquiry. Nothing is sent, ordered or reserved. Contact La Smorfia directly for confirmation.

Where can I check prices and dietary information?

Check the restaurant’s official website or contact them directly for the current menu, prices, ingredients and allergen information. Do not rely on the illustrative photographs to identify dishes or ingredients.

How do I send my selection?

Add the exact dish names from the current menu, tick the items you want to include and choose Copy selection. Open your email, paste the text, add your question and review it before sending. The website never sends it automatically.
