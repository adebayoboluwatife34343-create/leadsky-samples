Talk to the school ↗
Who is Jill Chaifetz Transfer High School for?

The school describes its community as serving students ages 16–21 seeking an alternative and responsive educational environment. Contact staff to discuss your situation and official enrollment requirements.

Where is the campus?

Jill Chaifetz Transfer High School is at the Forest Avenue Campus, 778 Forest Ave, Bronx, NY 10456. View the address on Google Maps. Confirm your visit with the office before travelling.

When can I visit?

Verified visiting hours and staff availability were not supplied for this concept. Select a preferred day in the visit planner, then call (718) 402-2429 to confirm a suitable time. The planner cannot reserve a visit.

What support does the school describe?

JCTS describes a rigorous classroom learning environment with individualized, student-centered support. Its mission includes earning a high school diploma and preparing for college, careers and post-secondary success, in partnership with Bronx Works.

Is this the official student portal?

No. This is an independent LeadSky product concept. All student names, schedules, grades, attendance figures, credit records and appointments are fictional sample data. No real student records, applications, bookings or AI services are connected.

What would a live platform need?

A live service would require the school’s approval, authenticated staff and student accounts, appropriate permissions, secure data storage, privacy and safeguarding review, verified admissions workflows, and integrations with approved school systems. Any AI recommendations would need clear limits and staff oversight.
