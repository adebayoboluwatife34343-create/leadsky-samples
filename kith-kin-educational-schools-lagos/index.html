A short demo guide
How do I try the complete journey?

Start at “Try an application” and use fictional details. Submit the local sample, then attach a dummy PDF or image under Documents & fees. Review a simulated payment; try the failure path and retry if you wish. In Admissions, open your record, review the attached file and accept or request changes. Finance shows the same simulated transaction. Switch to Parent to see the linked status and write an unsent message, then switch to Teacher to add a scripted reply. Filter the journal and open a photograph to follow its linked activity.

What is real, and what is simulated?

The school name, four school names, Lagos location and public contact details come from the supplied KKES information. All applicants, academic records, messages, documents, fee amounts and transactions are fictional or illustrative. Nothing is submitted, uploaded, paid or delivered to KKES. Data lives in memory for this browser tab; refreshing or resetting clears your changes. Stock photographs do not show KKES people or premises.

What would a live KEMS need?

A production system would need verified admissions requirements and fees, authentication, role permissions, secure document storage, a payment provider, audit trails, privacy safeguards and verified interfaces to existing portals. The separate entry points on the public website do not establish that the school's current systems are disconnected or manual. Less duplicate entry, clearer reconciliation and fewer status enquiries are proposed benefits, not measured results.
