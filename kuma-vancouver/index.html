Our Philosophy

Honoring Time-Tested Culinary Traditions

At Kuma Japanese, every dish reflects a deep respect for seasonal ingredients, knife skills, and authentic flavor profiles. Located at 3446 Dunbar Street, our kitchen combines classic Japanese culinary techniques with the finest local Pacific seafood.

Whether you are joining us for a quiet weeknight dinner or an immersive weekend omakase experience, our dining room provides a warm, inviting atmosphere designed for genuine culinary appreciation.

Fresh Daily Sourcing

Pristine seafood delivered daily to ensure exceptional flavor and texture.

Artisanal Broths
