Traditional Craft & Warmth

An Intimate Japanese Culinary Haven in Vancouver

Located on Dunbar Street, Kuma brings the soulful comfort of authentic Japanese izakaya culture to Vancouver. Our kitchen balances time-honoured culinary techniques with fresh local ingredients and seafood.

Gather around our tables to share small plates, enjoy binchotan charcoal grilled robata, and toast with premium junmai sakä in a serene, lantern-lit atmosphere.
