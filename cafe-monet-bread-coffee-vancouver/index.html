Our Heritage

French Baking Tradition on Robson Street

At Café Monet Bread & Coffee, we believe in slow fermentation, unbleached flours, and respect for classic French technique. Every morning before dawn, our ovens fire up to fill the air with the unmistakable aroma of warm butter and caramelized crusts.

Whether you are pausing during a busy day of shopping on Robson Street or settling in with a remote workspace journal, our doors are open to offer you a genuine European sanctuary in Vancouver.
