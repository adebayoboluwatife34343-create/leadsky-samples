Our Neighborhood Sanctuary

Rooted in Vancouver with a Passion for European Baking

Located right on Robson Street, Cafe Monet Bread & Coffee brings together the art of slow-fermented artisanal bread, comforting specialty espresso drinks, and refreshing traditional shaved ice desserts.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick morning flat white and fresh butter croissant on your way to work, picking up a warm sourdough loaf for dinner, or gathering with friends for a comforting bowl of shaved ice, our doors are open daily.
