About Olas blommor

A Stockholm florist for every occasion

Olas blommor is a florist in Stockholm. We hand-tie fresh, seasonal bouquets for delivery or collection across the city.

Whether you need flowers for a celebration, a wedding, or a sympathy arrangement, we are here to help. Call or send us a message to place an order.
