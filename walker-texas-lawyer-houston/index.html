General information

What to do after an accident.

Short, plain-English steps. General information only, not legal advice.

Move to a safe place if you can and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Get checked by a medical professional, even if you feel fine. Some injuries show up later.

Take photos of the vehicles, the road and any visible injuries. Note the time, place and weather. Collect names and contact details of other drivers and witnesses.

Share your name, contact details and insurance information. Avoid guessing about fault or apologising at the scene.

Insurers may ask for a recorded statement. Consider speaking with a lawyer first. You can ask for the Walker Texas Lawyer team on (713) 903-2481.

Keep medical bills, repair estimates, the crash report number and messages with insurers in one place. The assistant's summary can be a starting point.

Injury claims have deadlines that vary by situation. In Texas, many personal injury claims have a two-year limit, but exceptions exist. Please verify with an attorney as soon as you can.
