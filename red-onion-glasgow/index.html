A Taste of Modern Scotland

Located right on West Campbell Street near Royal Exchange Square, Red Onion has long been a cherished dining destination for theatre-goers, business diners, and food lovers alike.

We combine a relaxed, stylish neighborhood restaurant atmosphere with world-class technique, championing the finest seasonal ingredients Scotland has to offer.

Meet the Patron

Chef John Quigley

Rock 'n' roll chef turned culinary mentor, Chef John Quigley brings soul, passion, and decades of professional kitchen mastery to every plate served at Red Onion.

Having cooked for rock legends around the world before returning to his native Glasgow, John’s philosophy is simple: source the absolute best Scottish produce, treat it with respect, and serve it in a vibrant, welcoming environment.

“Good food is about passion, provenance, and sharing great moments with people you love.”
