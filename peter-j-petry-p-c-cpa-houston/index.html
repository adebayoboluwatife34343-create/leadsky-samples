Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your tax preparation or accounting engagement with Peter J. Petry, P.C., CPA.

Where is your office located in Katy?
Our office is conveniently located in LaCenterra at 2717 Commercial Center Blvd, Suite E200, Katy, TX 77494. We serve individuals and businesses throughout West Houston and Katy.
What should I bring to my initial tax consultation?
For individual tax preparation, please bring your W-2s, 1099s, prior year tax returns, and records of any significant deductions or expenses. For business returns, please bring your profit and loss statements and balance sheets.
Do you assist with IRS audits and back taxes?
Yes. We offer comprehensive IRS problem resolution services including IRS audit assistance, levy and lien reduction, unfiled tax returns, and installment agreements. Give us a call to discuss your situation confidentially.
Are you certified for QuickBooks consulting?
Yes, Peter J. Petry is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor offering consulting and training services by phone, email, or in-person for small business owners.
