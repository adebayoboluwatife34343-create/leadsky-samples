Swedish Craftsmanship

Baking with Pride in Stockholm

Every morning before sunrise, our ovens are warmed to bake traditional Swedish pastries and sourdough loaves using locally milled, organic flours. We believe in unhurried craftsmanship, simple wholesome ingredients, and the comforting ritual of fika.

The Atmosphere

A Peaceful Corner in Stockholm

Café Blåbär was created as a quiet retreat from the rhythm of the city. With warm blonde birch wood, natural morning light filtering through large glass windows, and the comforting aroma of freshly ground cardamom and artisan coffee, our space welcomes remote workers, students, and neighbors alike.
