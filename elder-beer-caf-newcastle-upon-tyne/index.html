Ask Elder a question ↗
Where can I find Elder Beer Café?

The supplied address is 290 Chillingham Road, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE6 5LQ. Open the address in Google Maps to explore the location and choose your route.

What are the opening hours?

The hours supplied for this concept are Wednesday to Sunday, 11:00–22:00. Monday and Tuesday hours were not supplied, so this website does not mark those days as closed. Current hours and bank holiday arrangements need confirmation directly with Elder.

Where can I find the current menu?

A current menu was not included in the supplied business information. Email eldercafe@icloud.com or check the business website for more information. The stock drinks photos in this concept do not represent a confirmed menu.

Does the planner make a booking?

No. Choosing a day only displays the opening information supplied for this preview. It does not contact Elder, reserve a table or confirm availability. Please email the café with any booking questions or specific requirements.

How can I check access or other visit requirements?

Please contact Elder at eldercafe@icloud.com before travelling to discuss your requirements. Accessibility, facilities and booking policies have not been verified for this concept.
