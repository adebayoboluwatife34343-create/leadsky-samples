Is this a real appointment or Cat Hotel reservation?

No. This independent concept demonstrates a proposed booking process. Every pet, availability slot, record and reservation in the portal is sample data. Nothing is sent to Riverclub and no appointment or accommodation is secured. Call the clinic to arrange a real visit.

Are these Riverclub's prices?

No. R 450 per veterinary appointment and R 180 per Cat Hotel night are illustrative demo rates chosen to demonstrate the estimator. Actual charges, accommodation policies and any treatment costs must be confirmed with the clinic.

What would need to happen before this could go live?

The clinic would need to approve the concept, imagery and content, confirm services, prices and policies, and connect secure booking, calendar, payment and notification systems. Authentication and permission-controlled patient records would require separate implementation. The demo does not provide medical advice.
