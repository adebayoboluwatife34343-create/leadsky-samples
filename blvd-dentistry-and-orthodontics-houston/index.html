How it works

Scheduling as easy as ordering takeout

That's how BLVD describes it on its own site. This concept makes it literal: one page, four taps.

  1. Choose an officeGalleria, Heights, Oak Forest, Richmond, Spring Cypress, 5th Street, Riverside or Hulen.
  2. Choose a treatmentCheck-up, orthodontics, children's visit or a toothache.
  3. Pick a timeSee open slots for that office, morning or afternoon.
  4. ConfirmThe office gets the request. You get a calendar file. In this demo, nothing is actually sent.

A small 3D tooth you can drag and turn. Just a friendly detail.

Fewer phone calls to the front desk

Patients currently call each office to schedule. Online booking can take routine requests off the phones so staff can focus on people in the building.

Requests that arrive after hours

Someone who looks at the website in the evening can book straight away, instead of waiting until a phone line is open.

FAQ

Questions before you book

Is this the real BLVD booking system?
No. It is an independent concept by LeadSky. Times and prices are samples, and nothing is sent to any office.
Do you recommend unnecessary treatment?
BLVD states on its website that it never recommends unnecessary services.
What about insurance and billing?
BLVD has an Insurance page on its website. A live version of this tool would show accepted plans for each office. We don't have those details here.
Can I book a child's visit or braces consult?
Yes, the demo includes both. BLVD offers care for the whole family and orthodontics.
What if I have a toothache?
The demo has a toothache option. For anything urgent, call your nearest office. Houston: (281) 205-0948. Austin 5th Street: (737) 990-4135.
What would a live version need?
A connection to each office's scheduling software, confirmation emails or texts, and approval from BLVD. None of that exists in this demo.
