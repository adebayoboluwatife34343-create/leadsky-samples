Business facts and evidence

Business information comes from the supplied Villa Angelia website text: locations in Ikoyi, Victoria Island and Accra; the five room categories shown; Deluxe Suite and Executive Deluxe features used in the assistant.

The supplied booking interface includes arrival, departure, location and room type. Its backend, current payment capabilities, CRM and operational impact have not been independently tested. This concept demonstrates a proposed workflow rather than a verified absence of existing systems.

No opening hours, address, cancellation policy or current room prices are asserted here.

What production would require
  • Verified room inventory and property-management integration.
  • A secure payment provider with location-appropriate currencies.
  • Consent-aware email and SMS delivery with approved templates.
  • Authenticated staff access, a CRM and secure guest-data handling.
  • Hotel-approved prices, taxes, cancellation terms and loyalty rules.
  • A production assistant grounded in approved hotel information.

Demo implementation and limitations

Browser-side validation, sample inventory, estimates, payment outcomes, message previews, chat intents, guest search, cancellation and loyalty share a single in-memory state. No external services are connected. Native date inputs provide the device's calendar.

Inventory assumes two rooms per category per location on each date, minus overlapping confirmed sample reservations. Each booking is for one room and at most two guests. Rates and inventory are entirely illustrative.

Responsive styling and reduced-motion rules are included. No independent browser, device or accessibility audit is claimed.
