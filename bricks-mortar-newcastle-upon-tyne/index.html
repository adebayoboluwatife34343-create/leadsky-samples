A few practical details

Before you come.

Where is Bricks & Mortar?

The supplied business address is 140 New Bridge Street, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE1 2SZ. Open directions in Google Maps and confirm your visit with the agency before travelling.

What time is the office open?

Opening hours have not been supplied for this concept. Call 0191 230 5577 to check the hours for the day you want to visit, including any holiday changes.

Does the visit planner book an appointment?

No. Choosing a date only helps you plan. It does not send your details, reserve a time or confirm availability. Contact the agency directly to ask whether you need an appointment.

Can I enquire without visiting?

Yes. Use the supplied telephone number, 0191 230 5577, or email hello@bricksandmortargroup.co.uk. These links open your phone or email application; this concept does not send an enquiry for you.

Is this the official agency website?

No. This is an independent website concept by LeadSky for Bricks & Mortar. It is a design demonstration, with no implied endorsement or existing client relationship. Business details shown come from the supplied brief and have not been independently verified.
