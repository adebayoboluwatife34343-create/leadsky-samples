Lush Tropical Sanctuary

A True Southeast Asian Escape on Davie Street

Step through our doors on Davie Street and leave Vancouver's bustling city streets behind. Surrounded by natural bamboo accents, lush potted palms, and warm ambient lighting, our dining room provides an immersive tropical atmosphere designed for gathering with friends and family.

Every dish we serve is prepared using authentic recipes and aromatic spices sourced directly from Southeast Asian markets, bringing genuine tropical warmth to the West Coast.
