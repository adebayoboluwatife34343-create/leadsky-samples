Our Neighborhood Sanctuary

Crafted for Connection in East Vancouver

Nestled right on Commercial Street, Chance Cafe was created to be a warm gathering place for the neighborhood. Whether you are dropping in for a quick morning flat white on your way to transit, settling in with a book by the sunny window, or grabbing a fresh loaf of sourdough bread for dinner, we are here for you.

We partner with independent coffee roasters who prioritize ethical farming practices and exceptional flavor profiles. Every cup is brewed with care, precision, and genuine hospitality.
