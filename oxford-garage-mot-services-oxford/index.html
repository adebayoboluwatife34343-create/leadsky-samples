Useful information

Before you
set off.

Where is the garage?

The supplied address is 4a Denmark Street, Oxford OX4 1QS. Open the address in Google Maps. Call the garage if you need help finding the entrance.

What time should I arrive?

Opening hours have not been verified for this concept. Call 07552 020236 to check the hours for your chosen day and agree a suitable time before travelling.

Does the visit planner book an appointment?

No. It lets you select a day and keeps the contact and directions links close by. Contact the garage directly to discuss availability. No appointment request or personal information is sent from this website.

What should I mention when I call?

Have your vehicle registration ready, explain what you need and mention any warning lights, sounds or changes you have noticed. Ask the garage to confirm the work they offer and how to arrange a visit.

Are these photographs of the garage?

No. All photographs on this independent concept are illustrative stock images. They do not show Oxford Garage & MOT Services, its staff, premises or completed work.
