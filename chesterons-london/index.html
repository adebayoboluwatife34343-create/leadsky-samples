06 Before you enquire

A few useful
answers.

What does “find your match” mean?

The quiz helps you organise a property enquiry around your intended move, timing and priorities. Its recommendation comes only from your answers. It does not search listings, assess eligibility or confirm which services the Chelsea branch can provide.

Has my enquiry been sent?

No. This is an independent website concept. The quiz runs in your browser and does not send your answers or contact the office. Copy your summary if you want to keep it, then call the office yourself.

What should I have ready when I call?

Your main question, the area or property you want to discuss, your preferred timing and any essential requirements. If you are discussing a particular listing, have its address or reference ready. The office can explain what other information it needs.

Are the photographs available properties?

No. All photographs on this concept are illustrative stock images. They are not presented as Chestertons listings, completed work, staff or premises. Interior and architectural-detail images are visual references and do not establish a Chelsea location.

Is this the official Chestertons website?

No. LeadSky created this independent concept to demonstrate a possible website experience. It is not endorsed by Chestertons and does not establish a client relationship. Use the official branch website or contact the office directly for current property and service information.
