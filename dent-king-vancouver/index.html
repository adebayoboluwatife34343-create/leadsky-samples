Common Questions

Paintless Dent Repair FAQ

What is Paintless Dent Repair (PDR)?
PDR is the art of meticulously massaging sheet metal back to its original contour from behind the panel using specialized rods and lights, eliminating the need for body filler or repainting.
Will PDR damage my factory paint?
No. Because we retain 100% of your original factory paint finish, your vehicle maintains its original UV protection and resale value.
Can all dents be fixed with PDR?
Most door dings, parking lot creases, and hail damage can be successfully repaired as long as the paint is not chipped and the metal is not excessively stretched or torn.
