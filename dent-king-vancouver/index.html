Methodology

Our 3-Step Restoration Process

01

1. LED Assessment

We analyze the dent depth, metal tension, and access points using specialized shadow boards and LED lines to map out the precise repair path.

02

2. Precision Pushing

Using custom-engineered stainless steel rods and tabs, our technician applies controlled pressure from behind the panel to gently ease metal back into alignment.

03

3. Final Polish

The restored area is polished and inspected under high-intensity light to verify optical clarity and ensure absolute perfection of the factory finish.
