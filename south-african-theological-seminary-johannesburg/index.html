Find a quick answer

When can I enrol, and how long does it take?
You can enrol at SATS at any time of the year. The supplied website text states that processing takes one week from application. This concept cannot check an actual application or predict a decision. Contact SATS for the status of a real application.
Do I need to attend a classroom?
SATS states that all its programmes are offered online. MySats is its online learning environment, described as the virtual classroom and central hub for study. This demo is a proposed companion workspace and has no live connection to MySats.
How are programme payments structured?
The supplied SATS website describes paying for courses or research phases as you are ready to take them, spreading the cost over your studies. The amounts you enter in this demo are illustrative and are not SATS fees. Check current pricing directly with SATS.
Which programmes can I explore?
Undergraduate options are the Bachelor of Theology, Higher Certificate in Christian Life and Higher Certificate in Christian Counselling. The BTh has an option to specialise in Counselling. Postgraduate options are BTh Honours, MDiv, MTh and PhD in Theology. The quiz suggests an option based on your interests; it does not assess admission eligibility. Review official entrance requirements with SATS.

The palette is a proposed interface direction, not a claim about official SATS brand guidelines.
