A few useful details

Before you go.

Dining reference photography · not the restaurant’s interior.

Where is Thai Flame?

The address supplied for this concept is 1902 Robertson Road, Ottawa, Ontario, K2H 5B8. Open the address in Google Maps and confirm the destination before travelling.

What are the opening hours?

The supplied hours are Monday to Friday, 11 am–2 pm and 5–9 pm; Saturday and Sunday, 5–9 pm. These hours have not been independently verified. Please call 613-695-9188 to confirm your visit, including holiday opening.

Does the visit planner book a table?

No. The planner displays the opening periods supplied for this concept. It does not check availability or make a reservation. Call Thai Flame directly to ask about visiting.

Where can I check the menu or ingredients?

Call 613-695-9188 or email info@thai-flame.ca to ask about the current menu, prices, ingredients and dietary requirements. The food photographs here are reference images, not a verified menu.

Is this the official Thai Flame website?

No. This is an independent, unendorsed website concept by LeadSky. Business details come from the supplied brief and have not been independently checked. The website listed in that brief is thai-flame.ca; its current availability has not been verified.
