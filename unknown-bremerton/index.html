Booking & Preparation FAQs

What is included in the booking price? +
All standard appointments include professional parting, protective styling, and neat finishing. Hair extensions can be provided or brought by client upon request.
How should I prep my hair before my appointment? +
Please arrive with your hair freshly washed, completely detangle-free, and stretched (blow-dried straight if possible) for the cleanest results.
Where are appointments located? +
All sessions take place in Bremerton, WA. Exact address details are sent automatically upon successful booking confirmation.
