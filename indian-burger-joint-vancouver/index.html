Crafted with Passion on Howe Street

Located right in the heart of Vancouver at 1020 Howe Street, Indian Burger Joint brings together the comforting art of American smash burgers and the vibrant, aromatic spice markets of India.

Every sauce is simmered from scratch, every spice blend is meticulously toasted, and our patties are smashed fresh to order every single day.
