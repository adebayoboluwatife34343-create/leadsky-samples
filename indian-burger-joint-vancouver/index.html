WHERE CLASSIC BURGER CULTURE MEETS INDIAN SPICES

Located right on Howe Street in downtown Vancouver, Indian Burger Joint was born out of a simple passion: taking the comforting, satisfying structure of a classic gourmet burger and charging it with the bold, rich, aromatic spices of traditional Indian cuisine.

Whether you are wrapping up a night out in the city at 1:00 AM or grabbing a satisfying dinner with friends, our kitchen delivers fresh smash patties, marinated tandoori chicken, and handcrafted mango shakes crafted daily.
