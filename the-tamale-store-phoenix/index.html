Rooted in Tradition in Phoenix, Arizona

At The Tamale Store, located right on North Cave Creek Road in Phoenix, we take pride in honoring time-tested culinary traditions. Every morning, our kitchen comes alive with the aroma of simmering chiles, fresh corn masa, and slow-roasted meats.

We believe in keeping things authentic, wholesome, and delicious for our local Phoenix community. Whether you are grabbing a quick lunch or picking up family packs for the weekend, every batch is prepared with genuine care.
