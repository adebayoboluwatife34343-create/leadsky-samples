Demo scope & integration requirements

This browser-only concept includes calculated sample comparisons, service filters, detail enquiries and a simulated appointment calendar. It has no insurer, AI, email, video, CRM or calendar connection. No insurance application, payment or booking is submitted. Live use requires approved API access, secure handling of customer information and confirmed business assets. The supplied business copy describes comparing insurer quotes; manual processing times and commercial impact have not been independently verified.
