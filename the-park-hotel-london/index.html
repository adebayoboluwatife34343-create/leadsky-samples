Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is this the hotel's official guest app?

No. This is an independent LeadSky product concept for The Park Hotel London. The hotel has not endorsed it. Guest names, dates, room assignments and queue activity in the app are sample data.

What actually works in this demo?

You can complete a sample check-in, simulate unlocking a room, chat with a scripted local guide, select room categories and create guest requests. Requests appear in the staff desk, where you can mark them handled. The discount calculator works locally. Nothing is sent, charged or booked, and demo records disappear when the page reloads.

What would a live version need?

A secure guest account system, a connection to the booking engine and PMS, identity verification and hotel-approved operating processes. Digital keys require compatible locks and secure credentials. Payments, notifications, live AI, loyalty and real room availability are not connected or implemented here.

Can I order breakfast or arrange late check-out?

The demo lets you enquire about breakfast and late check-out. It does not confirm availability, times, menus or prices. Contact the hotel's 24-hour reception for actual arrangements. No in-room dining service is represented as an existing hotel facility.
