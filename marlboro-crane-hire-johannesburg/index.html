Information & Safety

Operational Guidelines & Technical Requirements

Key guidelines for operating heavy crane equipment on South African job sites.

What site preparation is required prior to crane arrival?
Ground surfaces must be level, compacted, and free of unmapped underground services. High-capacity steel outrigger spreader plates or heavy timber mats are deployed based on site bearing pressure calculations.
Are certified crane operators and riggers provided with hires?
Yes. Marlboro supplies certified crane operators and qualified riggers in accordance with South African statutory Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) regulations and SANS codes.
How are permit requirements for abnormal load transport managed?
Our heavy transport division handles provincial movement permits, route clearance, and escort logistics for moving counterweights and crawler crane components across provinces.
How are safe working load capacities determined?
Every lift calculation incorporates total load mass, rigging tackle deduction, working radius, tip height, and wind conditions against OEM load charts.
