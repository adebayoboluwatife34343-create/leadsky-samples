    Is this a real booking system?

    No. This independent product sample demonstrates an intake and scheduling journey. All times and patient records are sample data. Nothing is sent to the practice.

    Can this tool tell me how urgent my symptoms are?

    No. The pathway labels demonstrate routing logic, not clinical triage. Contact the practice for professional assessment. For trouble breathing, difficulty swallowing, or severe uncontrolled bleeding, call 911.

    What would a live version need?

    An approved connection to the practice's scheduling and patient-management software, clinician-approved routing and preparation content, privacy review, secure data handling, and staff access controls.

    Does the demo store my information?

    The demo keeps entered details only in this page's temporary memory. It does not submit them or save them to local storage. Use fictional information and refresh the page to clear the session.

    Are the photographs of Palmer Dental Group?

    The logo is a business-published asset. The clinical photographs are illustrative stock images, not photographs of this practice's staff, premises, or completed treatments.
