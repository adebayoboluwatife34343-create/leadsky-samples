Does this book a real appointment?

No. Every slot is sample availability. Nothing is sent, reserved or added to the practice's systems.

Can this diagnose a dental problem?

No. It demonstrates administrative routing only. A qualified clinician must assess symptoms and decide on treatment. For a dental emergency, call the practice. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

What would a live version need?

A secure connection to the practice's scheduling software, approved intake rules, patient consent, privacy controls and clinician-reviewed messages.
