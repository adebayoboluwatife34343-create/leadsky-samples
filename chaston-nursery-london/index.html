A few helpful answers

Little questions.
Clear answers.

For actual admissions, fees and care arrangements, please contact Chaston Nursery directly.

info@chastonnursery.co.uk ↗
Is this the official Chaston Nursery website?

No. This is an independent, private product concept by LeadSky. It demonstrates possible digital services and is not endorsed by Chaston Nursery. Visit the official website for nursery information.

What ages does Chaston Nursery care for?

The supplied nursery information states that Chaston provides care and education for children aged 3 months to five years. Contact the relevant setting about suitable rooms and available places.

Can I book a real place or pay here?

No. All availability and booking actions are demonstrations. No booking is made and no payment is taken. Actual fees, sessions and places must be confirmed directly with the nursery.

Are the daily reports real?

No. Every child name, activity, attendance record and learning note in the portal is fictional sample data. A real service would require secure authentication, appropriate permissions and a connected nursery management system.

How would reminders and payments work?

A production platform would need approved payment and messaging providers. This concept only previews those steps. It does not send emails, SMS messages or reminders, and it never asks for card details.
