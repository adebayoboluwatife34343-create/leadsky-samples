04 / Before you book

A few things
to know.

Visit Cardi’s real profile ↗
Can I book a real appointment here?

No. This is an independent website concept by LeadSky. The bag and checkout demonstrate a possible booking experience. No appointment is reserved, no payment is taken and no details are sent. For a real appointment, visit @dfw_cardi71 on TikTok and follow Cardi’s booking instructions.

Are these Cardi’s actual prices?

No. Every displayed price is a sample for this concept. The currency selector changes the demo currency, not an exchange rate. Cardi would set the real services, prices, currency and payment terms before any live website launched.

How do I choose the style, length and hair?

Use the work gallery as a visual reference, then confirm the style, length, colour and whether hair is provided directly with Cardi. This concept does not assume which specific styles or lengths are available.

How should I prepare for an appointment?

Ask Cardi for the preparation instructions for your chosen style, including washing, detangling and any hair or products you need to bring. No preparation policy has been supplied for this concept.

Where are appointments, and what times are available?

A confirmed location, opening hours and live appointment calendar have not been supplied. Contact Cardi through the linked TikTok profile before making any travel plans. Dates in this demo are preferences only.

What about aftercare or changing a booking?

Confirm aftercare advice, maintenance guidance, deposit requirements and cancellation or rescheduling terms directly with Cardi. This concept does not create or apply booking policies on Cardi’s behalf.
