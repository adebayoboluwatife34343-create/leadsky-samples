04 / Before your hair day

A few things
to know.

Clear answers before you try the demo. Real appointment details should always be agreed directly with the stylist.

Can I book a real appointment here?

No. This is an independent website concept by LeadSky. The cart and checkout demonstrate a possible booking experience. They do not reserve a slot, take payment or send an appointment request. For a real appointment, visit the creator’s TikTok profile and use their published contact route.

Are these the stylist’s actual prices?

No. Every package and add-on uses a sample price for this demonstration. Package names, service availability, currency and final prices must be set and approved by the business before a real booking website launches.

How should I prepare my hair?

Ask the stylist for preparation instructions for your chosen look. Confirm whether hair should be washed, detangled or stretched, and whether extensions are supplied. This concept does not establish the stylist’s preparation policy.

Where is the appointment, and what are the hours?

A verified appointment address and opening hours were not supplied for this concept. Confirm the location, available times and travel arrangements directly with the stylist before making a real booking.

What if I need to change my appointment?

For a real booking, ask the stylist about deposits, changes, cancellations and late arrivals before paying. No cancellation or refund policy is implied by this demo. Demo cart items can be removed at any time.

Are all the pictures the stylist’s work?

The playable TikTok reels show the creator’s published videos, and the profile image comes from their public profile. The opening photograph is illustrative stock photography. Package images are original illustrations. Neither the stock photograph nor the illustrations are presented as the stylist’s completed work.
