Information

Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations for smooth appointment booking.

Hair care & protective preparation guidelines.
How do I book an official appointment?

Bookings are handled directly via Direct Message on TikTok (@dfw_cardi71). Use the planner on this website to generate a clear request message, then paste it into your DM with Mïşş CãrDï.

Where is the hair studio located?

The exact studio address and arrival details are provided directly to clients once your appointment date, time, and deposit (if required) have been confirmed.

How should I prepare my natural hair before arriving?

Hair should generally be freshly washed, thoroughly detangled, and blow-dried smooth with no heavy oils or extra products added, unless specified otherwise by Mïşş CãrDï.

Is hair extensions included in the service price?

Hair inclusion depends on the chosen style. Please specify your desired hair color and length in your DM enquiry to confirm if braiding hair is provided or if you should bring specific packs.

Does this website accept payments or lock in times?

No. This site is a private digital concept demonstrating website design for Mïşş CãrDï. No payment processing, live calendar integration, or binding contract is hosted on this demo page.
