Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting treated at Airrosti Cinco Ranch.

Are Airrosti treatments covered by insurance?

Yes, Airrosti treatments are covered by most major insurance carriers. Patients can check their specific coverage online or contact our patient services team prior to their visit.

How many visits are typically required per injury?

Airrosti focuses on rapid pain resolution, averaging just 3.2 visits per injury. Our combination of targeted manual therapy and active rehab is designed to resolve pain quickly.

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

In most cases, patients can schedule directly without a physician referral. Use our online booking widget above to select your preferred date, time, and Katy practitioner instantly.
